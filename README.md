# Support Stack Review

Chatbase sells one agent that answers across chat, email and voice. This is an outside read on where that holds up and where it does not.

**Read the full page:** https://chatbase-dev.github.io/

Support teams drowning in repetitive tickets are the obvious buyer, and the enterprise logos on the homepage suggest the platform survives contact with large, cautious organisations. Small teams without documented answers will get less out of it, because an agent trained on a thin knowledge base produces confident nonsense and a support queue full of escalations. The caveat nobody puts on a pricing page is that deployment is mostly content work, not configuration. And if the thing actually blocking your launch is the site the agent is supposed to sit on, that is a different problem, solved by something like Begin.sh rather than by a chatbot vendor.
